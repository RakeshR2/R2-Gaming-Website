Our Story

About R2

Built on Service. Driven by Partnership.

Customer Service is the cornerstone of the R2 Group of Companies. This foundational principle drives WHAT we do, HOW we do it, and most importantly WHY we do it.

We see ourselves as extensions of our partners' business — be it a manufacturer, supplier, or customer — bringing value to all sides of every relationship.

Today the R2 Group includes R2 Gaming, Lang's Ventures Inc, and R2 Technology Solutions. With offices in British Columbia, Alberta, and Ontario, the R2 Group provides support coast-to-coast.

Group of Companies

The R2 Group

R2 Gaming

Founded 2005 · Pan-Canadian

R2 Gaming is a Canadian company which specializes in distribution and services to the Casino Gaming, Lottery and Bingo Industry across Canada. Founded in 2005, R2 Gaming provides product sales, field installation, preventative maintenance, technical training, product staging, warehousing, logistics, product assembly and in-house electronic repair services to Crown Agencies and Gaming Operators in all Provinces across Canada.

Lang's Ventures Inc (LVI)

Est. 1998 · Kamloops, British Columbia

Based in Kamloops BC, LVI specializes in creating and testing technology solutions, field installation, and repair of all equipment relating to the casino, lottery, and bingo industry. Servicing 3,500+ lottery retailer locations and managing over 71,000 pieces of lottery equipment in the field.

R2 Technology Solutions

Strategic Partnership · North American Market

R2 Technology Solutions was formed through the strategic partnership between R2 and Eurocoin Components Inc. Bringing to the North American market custom and off-the-shelf technology solutions, as well as the supply of equipment, parts and supplies.
